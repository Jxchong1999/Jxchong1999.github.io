About

I am a master's student in Computer Science at National Tsing Hua University, working with Prof. Chiou-Ting Hsu. My research studies how face anti-spoofing systems can stay reliable when the attacks, sensors and environments they meet at test time differ from those seen in training.

My work spans domain-generalized feature learning, one-class learning from live faces only, and multi-modal learning across RGB, infrared and depth. It has appeared at CVPR, AAAI, BMVC, ICME and ICIP, and in Pattern Recognition, Information Sciences and Expert Systems with Applications. My interest in face security began with deepfake detection during my undergraduate studies at Multimedia University, Malaysia.

I am applying to PhD programs to continue research on trustworthy and generalizable computer vision.

  • Face anti-spoofing
  • Domain generalization
  • Out-of-distribution detection
  • Multi-modal learning
  • Vision-language models
